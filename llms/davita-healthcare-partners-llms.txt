# DaVita HealthCare Partners

> DaVita HealthCare Partners is the legacy name for the combined entity formed when DaVita Inc. merged
> with HealthCare Partners in 2012. The HealthCare Partners medical groups and physician networks were
> divested in 2019 and now operate as Optum California; healthcarepartners.com redirects to Optum. The
> surviving company, DaVita Inc. (NYSE: DVA), is a Fortune 500 kidney care provider running in-center
> hemodialysis, home dialysis, transplant support and integrated kidney care programs. Neither the
> combined entity nor its successors publish a public developer API, developer portal, SDK, webhook
> surface or machine-readable contract.
